# Company marks

File names match lowercase company names with spaces replaced by hyphens. The `.dark.svg` suffix supplies an optional dark-theme variant. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.

- [Amazon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_icon.svg) and [Bloomberg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bloomberg_logo.svg) use transparent Wikimedia Commons artwork. Dark variants use white in place of black.
- [Goldman Sachs](https://www.goldmansachs.com/) and [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/) use foreground paths from their site icons. Their decorative background rectangles are omitted. [Optiver](https://www.optiver.com/) uses its original transparent site icon.
- Additional brand paths come from [Simple Icons at 801eaab](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/801eaab5b70f4933137069397ebbd2ce5f9ad56e/icons), finished in brand colors with dark variants where needed. Visa uses the blue and white specified in its [brand standards](https://corporate.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/corporate/about-visa/documents/visa-brand-standards-sept2025.pdf). See [SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md](SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md).
- The remaining marks come from [SVGL at d11faf1](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/tree/d11faf16e695c805b87920c496a1746401e23e4b/static/library). See [SVGL-LICENSE.txt](SVGL-LICENSE.txt).
